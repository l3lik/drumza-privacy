Drumza Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Drumza is a drum notation app for iPad. This policy is short because we collect nothing.

What data Drumza collects

None.

  • Drumza does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.
  • All notepads, measures, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Drumza makes no network connections. It works fully offline.
  • Drumza contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • Drumza does not require an account.

Data you create

Your drum notation data stays on your iPad. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. PDF files you export are created locally and shared only where you choose to send them via the iOS share sheet.

Children's privacy

Drumza is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Drumza adds features that involve data (for example, optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated accordingly.
