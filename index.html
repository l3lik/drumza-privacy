1. Who we are

Drumza is a drum notation product. On web, you can sign in and sync notepads in the cloud. On iOS Phase 1, you can sign in with Apple or Google; your charts remain primarily on-device until a later sync release.

We do not sell your personal data.

Analytics: No product analytics or telemetry SDK is enabled in the web app today. iOS Phase 1 does not add a separate product-analytics SDK. Hosts (e.g. Cloudflare, Supabase) may still collect standard operational logs.

3. Processors / subprocessors (high level)

  • Supabase — authentication (and web database)
  • Cloudflare — web hosting / delivery
  • Apple / Google — if you choose their sign-in

4. Sharing (web)

If you add someone’s email to a notepad’s share list on web, that person may read the shared notepad. You control those lists.

5. Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. Web notepad data is kept while stored in our database. iOS local charts stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall (device OS rules apply). For deletion of account/auth data, email dimchik321@gmail.com — we handle requests in good faith. Automated self-serve delete may arrive later.

6. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or higher age required in your country). Do not use it if you are under the applicable age.

7. International transfers

Data may be processed in regions where our providers operate.

8. Your rights / choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us. On iOS, you can Sign out from the profile menu anytime. This policy does not claim full GDPR certification.

9. Android

Android may differ; separate notice when auth ships there.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy with a new version id. Material changes that affect the web Terms accept gate will be paired with a Terms version bump when required.
