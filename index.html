1. Who we are

Drumza is a drum notation product. On web and iOS, when you are signed in, your notepads can sync across devices and platforms through our cloud backend (Supabase) under your account (staging and production environments as configured for that build).

2. Data we process

We do not sell your personal data.

Payments: No paid features, in-app purchases, or card processing at this version.

Analytics / crash: No product analytics or telemetry SDK is enabled in the web app today. iOS does not add a separate product-analytics or crash-reporting SDK for this release. Hosts (e.g. Cloudflare, Supabase) may still collect standard operational logs.

3. Processors / subprocessors (high level)

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud database (including synced notepad content and web Terms acceptance records)
  • Cloudflare — web hosting / delivery
  • Apple / Google — if you choose their sign-in

We do not use a transactional email provider (e.g. Resend) for product sign-in, because email magic-link / OTP is not a current sign-in path.

4. Cookies (web)

We use essential / session cookies and similar storage needed to keep you signed in and run the web app. We do not use advertising cookies. You can block cookies in your browser; some sign-in features may then stop working.

5. Sharing

If you add someone’s email to a notepad’s share list on web, that person may read the shared notepad. You control those lists.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect safety or enforce our Terms, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer (in which case we will take reasonable steps so the recipient honors this Policy).

6. Retention

We keep account data, synced notepad data, and web Terms acceptance records while your account is active (or as needed to show prior acceptance). On-device copies remain until you delete them or uninstall (device OS rules apply). For deletion of account/auth/cloud notepad data, email dimchik321@gmail.com — we handle requests in good faith. Automated self-serve delete may arrive later.

7. Security

We rely on industry-standard protections from our providers (including encrypted transport and access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or higher age required in your country). Do not use it if you are under the applicable age. If you believe a child provided us data, contact us and we will delete it in good faith.

9. International transfers

Data may be processed in regions where our providers operate.

10. Your rights / choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us. On iOS, you can Sign out from the profile menu anytime. This policy does not claim full GDPR certification. We do not sell personal information (including under CCPA “sale” as we understand it); California residents may contact us for requests.

11. Android

Android may differ; separate notice when auth/sync ships there.
