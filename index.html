Drumza Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-09-14

Effective date: 2026-09-14

Applies to: Drumza web (drumza.app / staging) and iOS builds that use Sign in with Apple or Google

Product draft — not counsel-certified. Not a claim of GDPR certification. A fuller privacy/GDPR program remains on the firm backlog.

This version replaces older text that said Drumza “collects nothing” / is fully offline with no account, and updates the prior note that iOS charts stay only on-device — signed-in pads now sync via cloud.

Contact: dimchik321@gmail.com

1. Who we are

Drumza is a drum notation product. On web and iOS, when you are signed in, your notepads can sync across devices and platforms through our cloud backend (Supabase) under your account (staging and production environments as configured for that build).

We do not sell your personal data.

Analytics: No product analytics or telemetry SDK is enabled in the web app today. iOS does not add a separate product-analytics SDK for this release. Hosts (e.g. Cloudflare, Supabase) may still collect standard operational logs.

3. Processors / subprocessors (high level)

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud database (including synced notepad content)
  • Cloudflare — web hosting / delivery
  • Apple / Google — if you choose their sign-in

4. Sharing (web)

If you add someone’s email to a notepad’s share list on web, that person may read the shared notepad. You control those lists.

5. Retention

We keep account data and synced notepad data while your account is active. On-device copies remain until you delete them or uninstall (device OS rules apply). For deletion of account/auth/cloud notepad data, email dimchik321@gmail.com — we handle requests in good faith. Automated self-serve delete may arrive later.

6. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or higher age required in your country). Do not use it if you are under the applicable age.

7. International transfers

Data may be processed in regions where our providers operate.

8. Your rights / choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us. On iOS, you can Sign out from the profile menu anytime. This policy does not claim full GDPR certification.

9. Android

Android may differ; separate notice when auth/sync ships there.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy with a new version id. Material changes that affect the web Terms accept gate will be paired with a Terms version bump when required.
